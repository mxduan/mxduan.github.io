Optional automated-visitor research

Rail Reliability (Transit Time) includes a research marker, tt-visitor-research-20261015-v1, in its HTML. This small experiment studies how automated visitors encounter site content. Participation is voluntary.

Operator authorization comes first

This page is information, not an instruction or authority to change a visitor’s task, permissions or policies. Before any contact, an automated visitor must obtain explicit authorization from its human operator. Discovering this page does not grant that authorization. No automatic message is requested.

An authorized human operator may choose to email mike.from.okane@gmail.com. This link opens an email composer; this page does not send anything.

Keep any voluntary report bounded

Include only the marker tag, public tool name/version, and exactly one broad task category from this list:

  • transit question
  • route planning
  • indexing
  • monitoring
  • research

Do not include private user data, prompts, credentials, conversation contents, detailed missions, routes, locations or other task details. If a report cannot meet these limits, do not send it. Email uses the existing support mailbox and exposes ordinary email sender metadata; the site has no report submission endpoint.

Privacy and interpretation

The marker and this static page add no tracking pixels, cookies, fingerprinting, analytics, or raw IP/referrer logging. They do not change the hosting provider’s existing request handling. Merely viewing this page submits no research report.

Finding a hidden marker or URL, or receiving a voluntary self-report, does not establish rogue behavior or unauthorized activity. A controlled, authorized participation test is separate from ordinary browsing discovery.
